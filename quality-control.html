Quality Control

Every unit erected and inspected before it is packed — and the audit numbers to prove it.

What "100% Full Inspection" Actually Means

Most factories describe their quality control in one sentence. Buyers who have been burnt by a container of rejects know that sentence is worth nothing without a procedure behind it. This page sets out exactly what happens to a Xinqiu tent between the fabric roll arriving and the container being sealed — so you can hold us to it.

1

Incoming Material Inspection

Every fabric roll, pole set, zipper batch and accessory carton is checked on arrival: fabric weight per square metre, coating adhesion, colour consistency against the approved swatch, and pole diameter/wall thickness. Material that fails is quarantined before it ever reaches a sewing line.

2

In-Process Checks

Cutting accuracy, seam alignment, heat-taped seam coverage and pole-end finish are checked at each station. Our technical team supplies CAD previews and material recommendations before production starts, so the first article is approved against a drawing rather than an opinion.

3

100% Post-Assembly Inspection

Every single unit — not a sample — is erected after assembly and inspected for structural integrity, stitching, zipper function, pole fit and overall finish before it is packed. This is the step that separates a documented QC system from a promise.

Where It Happens

Ningbo — 3,300 m² (R&D and final inspection)

Our own plant houses product development, sampling, tooling and final inspection. New structures are proven here before a single commercial order is accepted, and every order is signed off here before shipping.

Anhui — 15,000 m² (mass production)

Volume production runs at our partner facility in Anhui under the same documented procedure and the same inspection checklist, which is how we sustain capacity of up to 20,000 tents per month without diluting the standard.

Wind Tunnel Testing

Structures are wind-tunnel tested to withstand 40–50 km/h, depending on model and anchoring system.

30 Minutes to Beilun Port

Our Ningbo site is 30 minutes from Beilun Port, which shortens the loading window and reduces total transit time.

Spare Parts Kit

Each container ships with a 1–2% spare parts kit (zippers, hooks) so retail partners can resolve field issues without waiting on a replacement shipment.
