 Every tent buyer asks about waterproofing, but few understand what the numbers actually mean. The **Hydrostatic Head (HH) rating**, measured in millimetres (mm), represents the height of a water column a fabric can withstand before leaking. A rating of 300mm means light drizzle protection; 1,000-2,000mm handles steady rain; and 3,000mm+ is the industry benchmark for serious outdoor shelters.
 
 
## Coating Systems: PA, PU and Silicone Explained

 The coating determines how a fabric achieves its rating. **PA (Polyacrylic)** coatings are cost-effective and lightweight, suitable for entry-level models. **PU (Polyurethane)** coatings add hydrolysis resistance, ideal for long-term UV exposure. **Silicone coatings** deliver the highest tear strength and UV stability but require seam sealing — the reason premium tents combine silicone-coated flysheets with PU-coated inner tents.  A fabric can be 10,000mm waterproof, but a single unsealed stitch line defeats it.
