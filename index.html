The Soul of Xinqiu

"We don't just manufacture tents; we protect the dreams of campers."

01 Driven by Problem-Solving

A few years ago, a prominent German distributor reported that most inflatable tents on the market suffered from air-column leaks in high-temperature environments. Instead of offering excuses, our R&D team spent two weeks in a 40°C testing workshop, experimenting with different TPU materials and heat-sealing techniques. The result? Our proprietary Stay-Rigid™ Air-Beam technology, which now ensures zero-leak performance even in extreme conditions.

02 Obsessed with Every Stitch

During a routine factory walk-through, our founder, Ray, noticed a senior craftsman meticulously re-inspecting an almost invisible seam seal. When asked why, the craftsman replied: "If this seal isn't perfect, a child might wake up damp in a midnight mountain storm. We can't let that happen to a family's dream." This became our standard: 100% internal QC, because we know the safety of your customers depends on our integrity.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ typically ranges from 500 to 1000 pieces per style for custom orders, depending on the material and structure.

Yes, as a professional manufacturer, we offer full OEM/ODM customization, including logo printing, color selection, and structural redesign.

Our average lead time is 30 to 45 days for mass production after sample approval and deposit receipt.
