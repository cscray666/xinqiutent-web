Company History — A Note on Our Registration Date

Our manufacturing operation began in 2006 as Ningbo Beilun Daqi Xinqiu Tent Factory. In 2011 the business was restructured and re-registered as Ningbo Xinqiu Travelling Products Co., Ltd.

Some company databases show only the 2011 registration date. Both are accurate: 2006 is when we started making tents; 2011 is when the current legal entity was formed. The team, factory and production know-how carried through the restructuring. We state this openly so there are no surprises during your due diligence.

Two Manufacturing Bases: Ningbo & Anhui

Ningbo (Zhejiang) — our own 3,300㎡ plant, BSCI audited, handling R&D, sampling, technical products and final quality control. Approximately 30 minutes from Beilun Port.

Anhui — 15,000㎡ of partner production capacity for high-volume runs, operating under our quality control and inspection standards.

Global Market Reach

Our outdoor tents are exported worldwide, with approximately 70% of our export volume going to European markets. Remaining volume serves North America, Oceania, Southeast Asia and the Middle East, driven by demand for camping, beach and privacy tents.

Fast growth is seen in Australia, UAE, South Korea, Malaysia and Brazil. We serve brand buyers, wholesalers, cross-border sellers and importers with stable bulk supply, OEM/ODM customization and on-time delivery.

Quality Assurance

At Ningbo Xinqiu, quality is our top priority. Supported by high ratings and positive feedback from customers, we implement strict raw material inspection, production control and finished product testing.

With BSCI social audit and standardized manufacturing, we deliver durable, safe and reliable outdoor tents to earn long-term trust from global buyers.
