Tent MOQ Guide

What minimum order quantity really means for a tent order — and how to make your first order work.

What determines a tent MOQ.

  • ›Fabric: coated and printed fabrics have their own mill minimums per color and pattern
  • ›Structure: pop-up and automatic-pole tents carry higher setup costs than standard pole tents
  • ›Customization: private-label printing and custom sizing raise the minimum
  • ›Accessories: custom bags, packaging and hang-tags add their own minimums

Why factories set MOQs.

  • ›Fabric is purchased in rolls — a mill won't produce a single roll of a custom color
  • ›Line changeover and QC setup are fixed costs spread over the whole run
  • ›Small runs push the per-unit cost above a sellable price

How to structure a workable first order.

  • ›Mix sizes and colors of one model to reach the minimum in a single run
  • ›Start with a standard model, then move to private label on the reorder
  • ›Plan a trial order + repeat order instead of one tiny run
