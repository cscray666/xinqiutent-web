The theoretical figure is discounted by the fill rate to allow for carton gaps, bulging and stacking losses. Final count is confirmed by trial loading.

Why we discount the volume

Pure volume (container m³ ÷ carton m³) assumes a perfect fit. In reality, cartons don't tessellate perfectly against the container's inner walls — there are corner gaps, carton bulging, and hand-loading losses. We apply a fill rate (default 90%) so the estimate stays realistic rather than optimistic.

Container inner volumes: 20GP ≈ 33.2 m³, 40GP ≈ 67.7 m³, 40HQ ≈ 76.4 m³.
