Institutional & Project Tents

Relief shelter, workforce camps and event fleets — engineered for repeatability and volume.

Tents Built for Programmes, Not Weekends

Relief agencies, contractors and event operators buy tents on completely different terms from retail: they need a documented specification, a repeatable unit, realistic lead times at volume, and a supplier who will still be there on the second and third order. That is the work our engineering team does best.

Relief and temporary shelter

Large-footprint structures for displacement camps and rapid-response deployments, specified for repeatability so that units from different production batches are interchangeable.

Workforce and site camps

Durable accommodation and welfare units for construction, mining and infrastructure projects in high-UV, high-dust and high-wind environments.

Event and rental fleets

Commercial gazebos, marquees and event shelters designed to be erected, struck and re-erected hundreds of times without structural failure — because fleet lifetime, not unit price, decides your cost per event.

Why Institutional Buyers Use Us

Capacity that holds

Up to 20,000 tents per month across 18,300 m² of manufacturing base — 3,300 m² in Ningbo for R&D and final inspection, 15,000 m² in Anhui for volume production.

Documented, auditable QC

Incoming material inspection, in-process checks and 100% post-assembly inspection, with amfori BSCI audit ID 156-060372-000 and SGS report 478583460_P+T; full copies are shared with verified buyers on request. See our quality control page.

Logistics that suit programmes

30 minutes from Beilun Port (Ningbo), which shortens the loading window and keeps staged deliveries on schedule.

Custom specification, not catalogue picking

Structures are engineered to your specification — fabric, coating, pole system, footprint and branding — with CAD previews before tooling. See how we work.
