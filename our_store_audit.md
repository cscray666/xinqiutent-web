## 5. Audit Summary & Gap Analysis

### What's Working Well:
- **Strong Visual Branding:** Consistent use of high-quality imagery and branding across all pages.
- **Social Proof:** Excellent integration of ratings, reviews, and verified status.
- **Clear Value Proposition:** "20+ years experience" and "Verified Supplier" status are prominently leveraged.
- **B2B Focus:** Detailed spec tables, tiered pricing, and clear MOQ data make it easy for buyers to evaluate.
- **Video Content:** Demonstration videos are high-quality and informative.

### Areas for Improvement:
- **Title Optimization:** While SEO-friendly, some titles are extremely long and could be more readable with better formatting.
- **Content Depth:** Adding more comparison charts (e.g., comparing different tent sizes/materials) could help buyers decide faster.
- **Mobile Experience:** Ensuring all infographics are easily readable on smaller screens (some text in images might be small).
- **Localization:** While multi-language is supported, some automatic translations in product titles could be refined for a more "native" feel.
