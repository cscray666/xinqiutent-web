How this works

FOB price — your ex-factory unit cost, including China-side inland transport to port.

Ocean freight — the container cost from Ningbo/Beilun port to the destination port. This is the volatile figure; enter your forwarder's live quote.

Import duty — the destination country's tariff on tent HS code 6306.22.00 (US 8.8%, EU 12%, UK 12%).

VAT / GST — destination sales tax on the duty-inclusive value (EU ≈19%, UK 20%, AU 10%, US 0% for B2B imports).

Port & clearance — destination-side customs brokerage, terminal handling and delivery.
